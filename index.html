University policies on LLM use in higher education

Oct 2025 - Mar 2026
  • 45Policy documents
  • 5Countries compared
  • 7Topics, final run
  • 0.59F1 score
  • 0.45Cohen's Kappa

What it does

An end-to-end NLP pipeline over 45 university AI-use policies drawn from five countries. Topic modelling with BERTopic and LDA surfaces what institutions actually write rules about; zero-shot classification labels the tone of each policy; chi-square tests and cosine similarity then compare countries against each other.

What I owned

I led the architecture and core implementation, shaped through discussion with my lab partners and professor: pre-processing and normalising all 45 documents in Python (tokenisation, lemmatisation, section and stop-word filtering), the BERTopic modelling, the country-level comparisons, and the final visualisations.

How it was validated

I hand-annotated a gold-standard subset and benchmarked outputs on precision, recall, F1 (0.59) and Cohen's Kappa (0.45): moderate agreement on a subjective tone-labelling task where institutional wording varies widely across countries. Reporting the honest number matters more than reporting a flattering one.
